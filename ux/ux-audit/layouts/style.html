2. Wireframes

Same chrome specimen three ways — pick one id and apply consistently.

Square · square

Technical, rigid, structural · border-radius: 0

Understand Evaluate Finding
Card surface
◐
Do
Zero radius on nav, chips, cards, theme control.
Don't
Mix rounded product frames inside square audit chrome inconsistently.

Squircle · squircle

Premium, continuously smooth · base border-radius: 16px + corner-shape: squircle; nest by φ≈1.618 → ~10px / ~6px. Fallback: same radii without corner-shape.

Understand Evaluate Finding
Card surface
◐
Do
Nest radii (16 → 10 → 6). Keep fallback radii when corner-shape is unsupported.
Don't
Use one radius for every nested control — nesting sells the squircle system.

Circle / Pill · pill

Dynamic, actionable · border-radius: 9999px (pills) or 50% (theme control).

Understand Evaluate Finding
Card surface
◐
Do
Pills for nav/chips; circle for the theme control.
Don't
Force huge pill cards that crush multi-line content — keep cards readable.
