1Core Philosophy

Purpose, emotional boundaries, and when to use this aesthetic.

  • Glass needs something rich behind it — never frost over flat gray.
  • Depth through translucency and specular edges, not heavy borders.
  • Keep text contrast; tint glass, don’t wash type out.

Real products

Apple.com · visionOS materials · Microsoft Fluent · Arc Browser · iOS Control Center

From our catalog

  • Closest systems: fluent-2, material-3, polaris
  • Also fits: visionOS / iOS materials, Windows Acrylic patterns

2Layout & Spatial Architecture

Canvas organization: grids, alignment, density — not arbitrary placement.

  • Floating frosted sheets over vivid or photographic grounds.
  • Stacked translucent toolbars and panels.
  • Soft separation; let blur do the hierarchy work.

3Typography Rules

Typeface choices, weights, and scale limits that build hierarchy.

  • System sans with optical sizing.
  • Medium weight UI; light large titles.
  • Avoid heavy serif clash.

Suggested fonts

  • UISF Pro / Inter — system clarity
  • AltGeist — modern UI

4Component Anatomy

Borders, corners, shapes, shadows, and icon language.

Border radius

squircle16px (+ corner-shape: squircle when available) · Premium continuous curves.
  • Large continuous radii; 1px luminous rim.
  • backdrop-filter blur + saturate; inset specular highlight.
  • Semi-transparent fills — never opaque white cards.

Icon rules

  • SF-style thin stroke on glass.
  • Subtle fill for selected state.
  • Match blur panel contrast.

Icon libraries

  • PrimarySF Symbols — best match
  • Web altLucide — thin stroke

5Intentional Color Systems

Limited spectrum; how colors interact for utility and emphasis.

  • Vivid mesh or photo underlay (always).
  • White/ice glass with cool blue tint.
  • Accents that survive blur (saturated blue / system blue).

6Do vs Don’t Matrix

Side-by-side guardrails — keep the Do, reject the Don’t.

Do
Frosted panels over color, soft radii, luminous rims.
Don’t
Opaque flat cards or glass over empty gray (nothing to refract).
