# Principles (Foundations overview)

Every page answers What / Why / When / How / When not before specimens.

## Produce

Decide the visual principles that make this product recognizable and that later foundations must obey.

- Derive from the brief and from the **overall tone** chosen in Ask first (surface, density, ornament, depth).
- Write 4–7 principles: what it looks like, and what it refuses.
- Each principle must steer color, type, shape, or density later.
- Put a short summary in `system.json` `principles`. Keep `tone` named in `system.json`.

## Represent

`principles.html`:

1. What / Why — one short intro
2. Looks like — principle cards with product language
3. Refuses — anti-patterns
4. When / How / When not — how to apply a principle when choosing tokens
5. Do / Don't strip

No token tables here. Foundations pages carry tokens.

## Do / Don't

**Do:** Keep principles few, concrete, and product-specific. Tie each to a later foundation.

**Don't:** Write vague adjectives (“modern,” “clean”) without a decision they force. Mix component or pattern rules into principles.
